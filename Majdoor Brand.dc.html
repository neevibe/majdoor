01 — PRIMARY LOCKUP · DARK SURFACES

The master wordmark: the infinity ligature is cut from the same face as the letters — matched weight, bevel and lighting — with the blue → violet → amber gradient, and the tagline set beneath in the brand accents. This artwork is for dark surfaces; the graphite (02) and monochrome (03) versions are derived pixel-for-pixel from the same master, so letterforms, spacing and the infinity ligature are identical across all three.
